📖 Welcome to KAPTAN-HETM Info Guide

KAPTAN-HETM is your ultimate Personal & Group Expense Manager, designed to streamline your finances and make group expense splitting effortless.

1. Accounts & Data Security

Registered Account (Secure & Permanent)

  • Requires permanent UserID, Password, and verified Mobile Number.
  • Your data is safely stored permanently. Logging out does not delete your data; to permanently delete your account and records, go to Profile Settings / Delete Account.

Guest Account (Temporary Session)

  • Quick access without registration for temporary use or demo.
  • ⚠️ CRITICAL: Data is automatically erased permanently once you logout.

2. Personal Expense Tracker

  • Add Expense: Record standalone personal expenses outside of groups.
  • Settlement Ledger Sync: Settlement credits received or dues paid are recorded as independent ledger items to keep your audit trail fully transparent.

3. Group Expense Manager & Splitter

  • Create groups, add members with exact matching names, and record shared expenses.
  • Use the **Settlement Calculator** to view individual balances.
  • Click **"Mark Settled & Clear Dues"** to finalize the settlement and synchronize records into your personal ledger.

4. Personal Monthly Budget & Analytics

  • Set monthly spending limits and monitor budget breaches through the interactive year/month analytics chart.
